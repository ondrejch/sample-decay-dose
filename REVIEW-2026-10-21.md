# Review: full repository at `5c61eee` (tracked files only)

- **Mode**: whole-repo review of code, physics and documentation.
- **Head**: `5c61eee` (Address REVIEW-2026-09-27).
- **Baseline checks**: `pytest -q` passes (294 tests). `pyflakes` reports only unused imports and
  placeholder-free f-strings.
- **Method**: one reviewer read the core library (`SampleDose.py`, `utils.py`, `read_opus.py`, `HotCell.py`,
  `Radiator.py`) against the SCALE 6.3.3 manual and a real F71 read with `obiwan`. Four parallel reviewers covered
  `leaky_box_origen`, `concrete_irrad` with the radiator and hot cell, `FluorideSaltMixer` with the nuclide data, and
  the examples with `gw_exe` and the repository metadata. Every finding below was re-checked by the main reviewer

- **Verdict labels**: CONFIRMED means the behavior was demonstrated. PLAUSIBLE means the reading is strong but
  SCALE-side behavior was not executed.

## Summary

The REVIEW-2026-09-27 items are fixed in `5c61eee`. The ORIGEN `retained` semantics of the MHA deck, the F71
distribution parameters, the `useNormConst` source strengths, the OPUS intensity units and the `gper` density sum
were checked against the manual and a real F71 and are right. The highest-value defects found now:

1. `ADENS_SS316H_HOT` was not SS-316H. It held Ni 0.12 wt% and Mn 0.02 wt% with Fe as the balance, a ferritic
   Fe-17Cr-2.5Mo steel. It is the default irradiation material and the default first tank layer.
2. The tank, hot-cell and radiator decks froze the problem boundary at construction, so a detector standoff larger
   than 10 cm placed the detector outside the geometry.
3. The leaky-box dose integrator evaluated the chi/Q schedule only at the ORIGEN time points. On the production
   grid the 0--2 h and 2--8 h windows fall between points, and the first-day dose came out 12% high.
4. The concrete lid model homogenized the rebar mat as two bar families sharing one bar-diameter slice, which
   removed 2.95% of the steel, and it had no upper slab, so the "lid top" detector sat on the rebar mat.
5. `FluorideSalt` gave enriched salts the mass density of the natural salt, accepted a single-isotope uranium
   weight-fraction dictionary as 100% U-235, and split elemental impurities by natural abundance even under an
   enrichment.

## A. Results-affecting findings

### A1. `ADENS_SS316H_HOT` composition (CONFIRMED)

- `sample_decay_dose/SampleDose.py:108-115` at `5c61eee`. Weight percent from the table: Fe 79.7, Cr 17.3, Mo 2.55,
  Ni 0.12, Mn 0.02, Si 0.01, no Co. The cold table is SS-316 (Fe 65.3, Cr 17.0, Ni 12.0, Mo 2.5, Mn 2.0, Si 1.0,
  Co 0.1). The cited source (Sandmeyer 316H) lists Ni 10--14 wt%.
- Used by `OrigenIrradiation.write_atom_dens()` as the default sample, by `DoseEstimatorSquareTank` as the default
  first layer, and by nine example scripts.
- Fix: the hot table is now the cold alloy at 873 K. The mean linear expansion coefficient of 316 over 300--873 K,
  18.8e-6 /K, gives a volume factor of 1.0327 (`SS316_VOLUME_EXPANSION_873K`), so the hot density is 7.52 g/cm3.
  `test_ss316h_compositions_match_the_alloy_specification` pins both tables to the alloy band.

### A2. Detector standoffs outside the boundary (CONFIRMED, deck level)

- `SampleDose.py` tank decks, `HotCell.py`, `Radiator.py`. `box_a` was set in `__init__` from the default standoff
  (10 cm, or handling standoff + 10 cm). A later `det_standoff_distance = 50` or
  `handling_det_standoff_distance = 100` produced a boundary of `4p 60.24` with a detector at `x = 120.24`.
- Fix: `DoseEstimatorSquareTank.boundary_pad()` returns `max(box_a, standoff + 10)`, and the handling estimators
  include the handling standoff. The decks use it in place of `box_a`, which stays the user's offset. The hot-cell
  grid gains planes around the handling detector. Test: `test_detector_standoffs_stay_inside_the_boundary`.

### A3. Leaky-box dose integration across chi/Q windows (CONFIRMED numerically)

- `leaky_box_origen/LeakyBox.py`, `compute_dose_timeseries`. The trapezoid used chi/Q at the row times only. The
  box-A grid of the 90 d run has first-day nodes at 0, 0.24 h, 12.24 h and 24.24 h, while RG 1.145 changes chi/Q
  at 2 h, 8 h and 24 h. For a constant release the first-day dose was 1.119 times the exact value; the 90 d total
  was unaffected.
- Fix: each row interval is split at the schedule boundaries and the DCF-weighted release sum is interpolated
  linearly, which is exact for a linear release under a stepwise chi/Q. Tests compare a coarse and a 1-minute grid
  against the closed form.

### A4. Leaky-box release semantics and defaults (PLAUSIBLE, documented method)

- `compute_dose_from_box` required the caller to supply `removal_rate_s`, and nothing tied it to the
  `0.1 * PCTperDAY` that `_run_simulation` applied to box B. The defaults `chi_q_schedule=1.0` s/m3 (300 times the
  RG 1.145 0--2 h value), `breathing_rate_m3_s=3.3e-4` and no immersion table did not match the journal method
  (RG 1.145 at 400 m, 22.2 m3/d, ICRP-72 immersion for the noble gases).
- Fix: `_run_simulation` writes `leaky_boxes_rates_<prefix>.json5` with the removal rates it applied, and
  `compute_dose_from_box` reads the rate of the requested box from it when `removal_rate_s` is None. The defaults
  are now `chi_q_schedule_rg145_400m()`, `BREATHING_RATE_M3_S = 22.2 / 86400` and
  `DEFAULT_ICRP72_IMMERSION_CSV`. Box C has no removal of its own; with `inventory_bq` it needs an explicit
  `removal_rate_s` and warns that `rate * A_C(t)` is the conservative surrogate the journal draft describes.
- A step whose F71 is missing now raises in the dose path instead of entering a zero row. The activity plot marks
  it NaN with a warning. `_run_prefix_from_box_json` keeps a prefix that contains a dot. The merged activity CSV
  no longer carries a stray `time [d]_C` column. The box B/C decks start their time list one step after `start`,
  so the eight positions no longer include a zero-length first step.

### A5. Concrete lid model (PLAUSIBLE physics, CONFIRMED code)

- Rebar mat: the two bar families of a tied or welded mat are stacked, so they share no volume and the mat is two
  diameters thick. The single-slice model with a Steinmetz subtraction removed 2.95% of the steel and with it of
  the Co-59 inventory. Fix: `layer_thickness_cm = 2 d`, `f_steel = pi d (sx + sy) / (8 sx sy)`. At the defaults
  the layer is 3.18 cm thick, the steel fraction 0.0615 and the mixture density 2.641 g/cm3 (was 1.59 cm, 0.1193,
  2.962). The steel mass per unit lid area is now the full mass of both families.
- Upper slab: the model ended at the rebar mat, so the "lid top" contact detector saw no concrete above the mat.
  Ten centimetres of concrete attenuate the uncollided 1.25 MeV flux to about a quarter. Fix: `top_thickness_cm`
  adds an unactivated concrete body (mixture 3, no source) between the mat and the detector. The default is 0,
  which keeps the earlier geometry, and the README states what the detector then measures.
- `run_activation()` reused a deck already on disk, and `case_dir` keyed only on name and day counts, so a second
  model with another flux or geometry ran the first model's deck. Fix: the deck is always rewritten and the case
  directory carries a hash of the ORIGEN inputs.
- The importance grid covered the lid footprint only. It now spans the problem boundary. `--lib-pos` selects the
  F33 cross-section set; ORIGEN used set 1 silently before, while `OrigenIrradiation` uses the last set.

### A6. FluorideSaltMixer (CONFIRMED)

- Enriched salts inherited the natural density: the pure molar volume was `M_enriched / rho_natural`, so a Li-7
  FLiBe had 0.16% lower Be and F number densities than natural FLiBe. Fix: molar volumes come from the natural
  molar mass, and the mass density scales with the enriched molar mass.
- `{'U': {'u-235': w}}` renormalized to 100% U-235, while the mole-fraction form raised. Fix: the weight fractions
  must sum to 1. `U235_enr` together with an explicit `U` entry raises instead of overwriting.
- An elemental impurity of an enriched element took the enriched mass with the natural isotopic split. Fix: it
  follows `isotopic_distribution()`. Negative impurity fractions raise. The generic formula fallback resolved
  `PUF3` as P + U + F3; it now accepts the fluoride of one element case-insensitively and rejects the rest.
  With `impurity_cutoff=0` every trace anion raised; anions below `ANION_IMPURITY_FLOOR` stay impurities.
- The BeF2 slope 1.45e-5 is ten times smaller than the 1.4771e-4 of some compilations. Ideal mixing of the
  pure-component molar volumes underpredicts the Janz FLiBe correlation by 2.9% at 800 K, 1.8% at 900 K and 0.8% at
  1000 K with the current slope, and by about twice that with the larger one. The value is kept and the comparison
  is documented at `SALT_COEFFICIENTS`. The `Li7_enr` weight-fraction convention is documented (99.995 at% is
  w7 = 0.999957).

### A7. Library and gateway hardening (CONFIRMED)

- `OrigenIrradiation` and `OrigenFromTritonMHA` stage the F33 and F71 with `os.path.join(cwd, path)`, so an
  absolute path works. `MHATank.scale_decayed_pipe_material()` changes into the ORIGEN directory by absolute path.
  `DoseEstimator.run_mavric()` takes `nmpi` like the tank estimators.
- `Origen.get_beta_to_gamma()` warns for a pure beta emitter, where the beta estimate (beta_over_gamma times the
  photon dose) is zero by construction.
- `atom_dens_for_mavric()` keeps the eight isomers that have their own SCALE evaluations (am-242m, ...) instead
  of giving Am-242m (141 y) the Am-242 (16 h) cross sections.
- `get_burned_nuclide_atom_dens()` and `get_burned_nuclide_data()` raise for an F71 position beyond the file; the
  empty dictionary they returned would have started a leaky-box sub-run from an empty box.
- `RadiatorBox` raises when the tube outer radius exceeds the half pitch or the wall thickness is not positive.
- `gw_exe`: `logs --follow` printed the tailed lines twice; a job left in meta state SUBMITTED counted as RUNNING
  forever; the launch SSH call had no timeout; `wait()` died on one transient SSH failure. All four are fixed, with
  `LAUNCH_TIMEOUT_SECONDS`, `ScalerteSshClient.timeout_seconds` and `max_consecutive_errors`.

- `examples/radiator/radiator_doses_decaytime.py` performed one run without a decay scan; it is renamed

  sample without Be or F; their docstrings and READMEs said FLi7Be. `aluminum/al_irr_dose.py` carried a leftover
  FLiBe block. `irradiator.py` reads `decayed_F71_position`. `storage_tank_plots.py` rendered every figure twice.
- `.gitignore` now covers `irr_*/`, `dose-*year_*g/`, `*.msg`, `*.plt` and the example PNG and XLSX outputs.
- The `unittest` runner collects only the `TestCase` modules (120 of the tests). `AGENTS.md` and `test/README.md`
  no longer present it as an alternative to `pytest`.
- `concrete_irrad/README.md` and `leaky_box_origen/README.md` describe the revised models, and
  `sample_decay_dose/README.md` documents the boundary pad, the isomer rule, the F71 guard and the SS-316H tables.

## C. Checked and found correct

- ORIGEN `retained` zeroes every unlisted element (manual 5.1.5.2.17), so the MHA deck keeps the six volatile
  elements only, as intended. Position 1 of a saved F71 is the start state (step 0).
- The Monaco `origensBinaryConcentrationFile` parameters are the F71 case (position) and the spectrum type, 1 for
  total neutrons and 5 for photons. `useNormConst` sets the strength to the distribution total.
- OPUS `units=intensity` is particles/(MeV s); `integrate_opus()` multiplies by the bin width.
- `obiwan -units=gper` sums to the mass density. On `examples/msrr.f71` the sum is 2.4987 g/cm3, equal to the
  `gram` total over the `volume` row. The CSV has no total row.
- The predefined materials have the expected densities (HDPE 0.94, concrete 2.30, lead 11.34, dry air 1.2e-3,
  kaowool 0.16 g/cm3) and are now pinned by a test.
- `isotopes.py` and `data/isotopic_data.json` agree entry by entry; abundances sum to 1 for every natural element.
- The DCF tables: all 2,500 nuclide names are well formed; about 60 anchor nuclides match ICRP-72 and FGR-11 to
  the printed precision.

### Open decisions

- `top_thickness_cm` defaults to 0, which reproduces the earlier concrete geometry. A lid with concrete above the
  mat needs the real thickness to get the dose at its top surface.
- The BeF2 density slope stays at 1.45e-5 g/cm3/K. Pass a corrected `SALT_COEFFICIENTS` entry if a measured value
  is preferred.
- The journal draft treats `eps * A_C(t)` as a conservative release-rate surrogate. The code accepts it with an
  explicit rate and a warning; the environmental release of the model is the box-B leak.
